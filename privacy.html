Privacy Policy

Floor is the Lava · Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information Floor is the Lava ("the game"), made by Raider Games (Maxim Maximov, "we"), collects and how it is used. We do not have player accounts and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, stars, settings and purchases are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on our servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location, information about ad views and taps, and app performance and crash data. On iPhone the game asks for your permission before using the advertising identifier, and in Europe and some US states you are asked for consent first. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can unlock it.

4. Multiplayer

When you play with friends, the game connects your device directly to theirs (peer to peer) through a public connection service (PeerJS). The name you choose, your character and your position in the game are shared with the players in your room. As with any direct connection, your IP address is visible to the other devices and the connection service. Nothing is stored after the game ends.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices and rights

You can turn off ad tracking in your device settings, reset your advertising identifier, buy Remove Ads, or delete the app to remove all saved data. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union) you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Contact us and we will help.

7. Security and changes

We keep data collection to a minimum. If we change this policy we will update the date above.
