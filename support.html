Support

Need help with Floor is the Lava? Email us at maxmaximov360@gmail.com. We usually answer within 2 business days.

Common questions

I bought something and it is not showing. Open the Shop, go to Deals and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android use your Google Play order history.

How do I remove ads? Buy Remove Ads in the Shop. Anyone who buys anything never sees ad breaks. Reward videos are always optional.

How do I play with friends? Main menu, Friends, Create lobby, then share the room code. Everyone needs the same game version.

The game runs slowly. Pause, Settings, Graphics: choose Auto or Low.

How do I delete my data? The game keeps your progress only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. To stop personalized ads, turn off tracking in your phone settings.

When you email us, tell us your device model, the game version (in Settings) and what happened.
